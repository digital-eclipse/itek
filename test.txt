Managed Security
Phishing & Spear Phishing
When it comes to healthcare organizations, phishing for data is the most common cybercrime. The goal is to obtain valuable information, such as bank account numbers or passwords. Phishing occurs when employees are contacted via email or phone by someone posing as a legitimate professional. Spear Phishing is the same concept, except attempts are more targeted, aiming for specific individuals.

Managed Security
Malware
Malware issues, which include Ransomware and Spyware, stem from someone clicking on a suspicious link or email attachment, allowing cybercriminals to access the organization’s critical files, including patient data, and applications the organization utilizes. Once this occurs, they can modify and delete files, limit your access and completely wipe out systems.
